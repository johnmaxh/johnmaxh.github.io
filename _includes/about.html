About Me

I'm a fourth-year PhD student at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Prof. Subhransu Maji. My research focuses on building reliable, statistically grounded AI systems for scientific discovery. In particular, I develop human-in-the-loop methods that combine AI models with principled sampling and estimation techniques to produce unbiased, data-efficient measurements at scale. More broadly, I am interested in how machine learning can extend scientific reach, especially in settings where labeled data is scarce or expensive

Before joining UMass, I received my Master's and Undergraduate degress from the University of Michigan, where I did research on Video Prediction and 3D Computer Vision. During that time, I worked with Professors Andrew Owens, David Fouhey, and Jia Deng.
