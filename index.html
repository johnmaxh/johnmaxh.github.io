How are samples and estimates calculated?

Model predictions. We use AI predictions to focus human review on the regions expected to be most informative. Crucially, the framework’s unbiasedness and confidence interval guarantees hold regardless of the accuracy or quality of those predictions. Better predictions lead to narrower confidence intervals.

Sampling. Uniform sampling ignores the model predictions and chooses equally among unseen regions. Our model-guided method samples without replacement using the underlying continuous model score; the count shown above is its rounded display value. A small exploration floor ensures every region can be selected, including those predicted as empty.

Estimation. Each model-guided draw uses an inverse-probability estimate of birds in the unseen image. Draw-level estimates are combined with fixed without-replacement weights from the Active Measurement paper. Intervals: uniform uses a finite-population variance and model-guided sampling uses the paper’s conditional variance construction.
